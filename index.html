09 // FAQ

PROTOCOL QUESTIONS

How large are the internal dimensions?

Internal bay fits US 14 / EU 48. Removable riser allows two smaller pairs stacked.

Is the UV-C emitter safe?

Lid-sealed interlock. The lamp only fires when the latch contact is closed.

What's the battery runtime?

5200 mAh internal cell. ~180 days standby, ~30 days of hourly UV cycles.

How do I clean the exterior?

Microfiber and isopropyl 70%. Avoid abrasives on the brushed finish.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently CIS, EU, UK, JP, US. Contact ops for other zones.
